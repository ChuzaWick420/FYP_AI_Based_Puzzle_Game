```plantuml
@startuml UC08_AdvanceToNextLevel
title UseCase08 - Advance to Next Level

actor Player
participant ":ResultMenu" as RM
participant ":InputHandler" as IH
participant ":Engine" as E
participant ":EventListener" as EL
participant ":StateMachine" as SM
participant ":MapsManager" as MM
participant ":Player Entity" as PE
participant ":Ai" as AI
participant ":PlayingScreen" as PS

note over E, PS
  Background: the level increment actually happens
  automatically the moment the player reaches the goal
  (PLAYER_WIN cascade below), before ResultMenu is
  ever shown to the player.
end note

E -> E : __handleGameEvents(PLAYER_WIN)
E -> EL : createEvent(LEVEL_NEXT)
E -> E : __updateDB()
E -> E : __updateCompletionTimes()

E -> E : __handleGameEvents(LEVEL_NEXT)
E -> E : session_data["current_level"] += 1\n(wraps to 1 past MAX_LEVELS)
E -> E : session_data["saved_mst"] = []
E -> EL : createEvent(REQUEST_LEVEL_GENERATE)

E -> E : __handleGameEvents(REQUEST_LEVEL_GENERATE)
E -> E : __initialize()
E -> MM : createGraph(new current_level)
E -> EL : createEvent(LEVEL_RESET)

E -> E : __handleGameEvents(LEVEL_RESET)
E -> MM : initialize()
E -> PE : init(mapsManager.getMazeMap())
E -> AI : init(mapsManager.path)
E -> PS : setLevel(new current_level)
E -> EL : createEvent(MAZE_UPDATE)
E -> PS : update_maze(mapsManager.getFrameBuffer())
note right of PS : Next level's maze is already cached in\nPlayingScreen, ready to display, while\nResultMenu is still what's on screen.

== Player is looking at the Result Menu ==

Player -> IH : click "Next Level"
IH --> E : processEvents() = MOUSE_CLICK
E -> EL : createEvent(MOUSE_CLICK)

E -> EL : getEvents()
EL --> E : [MOUSE_CLICK]
E -> E : __handleButtons()
note right of E : currentState == RESULTS - every\nbutton here also fires LEVEL_RESET
E -> RM : getButtons()
RM --> E : [Next Level button]
E -> RM : button.getInput()
E -> EL : createEvent(LEVEL_RESET)
E -> SM : stepState(input)
E -> EL : createEvent(STATE_TRANSITION)

E -> EL : getEvents()
EL --> E : [LEVEL_RESET]
E -> E : __handleGameEvents(LEVEL_RESET)
note right of E : Re-syncs Player/Ai/MapsManager to the\nalready-selected next level (redundant\nbut harmless)

E -> EL : getEvents()
EL --> E : [STATE_TRANSITION]
E -> E : __handleMenuTransitions()
E -> SM : getNextState()
SM --> E : GameStates.PLAY
note right of E : No branch matches PLAY -\ncurrentState flips to PLAY.

E -> PS : render(screen)
note over PS : The next level's maze - generated back\nwhen the player originally won - is displayed
@enduml
```
